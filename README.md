# QashqAI Voice

**QashqAI Voice** is a cultural-technology initiative dedicated to the preservation, revitalization, and digital future of endangered and underrepresented languages through ethical, community-centered artificial intelligence.

Founded by **Siefollah Aslani**, QashqAI Voice begins with the Qashqai language as a living cultural and linguistic foundation, while developing a replicable framework for endangered languages worldwide.

---

## Mission

QashqAI Voice exists to protect endangered languages and cultural memory from digital erasure.

The project works at the intersection of:

- endangered language preservation
- speech and voice technologies
- cultural heritage and oral history
- digital inclusion and accessibility
- ethical and responsible AI
- community-centered cultural data governance

---

## Why It Matters

Thousands of languages remain digitally invisible.  
When a language disappears from digital systems, it risks disappearing from education, technology, and eventually intergenerational memory.

QashqAI Voice responds to this challenge by building ethical, inclusive, and culturally respectful AI pathways for languages that have long been marginalized in mainstream technological development.

---

## Project Vision

QashqAI Voice is not only a language project.  
It is a broader framework for:

- preserving endangered linguistic heritage
- strengthening cultural dignity in the digital age
- supporting multilingual inclusion
- developing responsible AI systems for underrepresented communities
- creating models that can be adapted for other endangered languages globally

## Foundational Principles

QashqAI Voice is guided by the following principles:

1. **Cultural dignity first**  
   Languages are not raw data. They are living carriers of memory, identity, and worldview.

2. **Community-centered participation**  
   Language communities must be treated as partners, not extractive data sources.

3. **Ethical AI under human oversight**  
   AI must serve people, cultures, and rights — not erase or exploit them.

4. **Transparency and responsibility**  
   All AI-assisted outputs should remain transparent, accountable, and open to human review.

5. **Global relevance through local roots**  
   The project begins with Qashqai, but its long-term purpose is to support endangered languages internationally.
